orivonstack.eth

The Orivon browser and the sites around it, published on IPFS and named on Ethereum. Open any of them in any browser through eth.limo, or in Orivon, which checks the name and every block on your machine.

Open them in Orivon

Paste an address such as ipfs://docs.orivonstack.eth into the address bar. A light client on your machine proves the .eth name, and every IPFS block is checked against its hash. Without Orivon yet, download it here.

How the names work

orivonstack.eth is an ENS name. Each name below it carries a content hash that points to a folder on IPFS, so the address names the content and not a server. In another browser, eth.limo reads the name for you and serves the folder. In Orivon your own machine does it, and checks the result.
